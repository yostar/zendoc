# Value Proposition

**Audience:** Average person. Non-technical.

**Reality:** The value prop is user-dependent. Hard to summarize. More of a "try it and you'll see" product.

---

## Things that make it intriguing to try

1. **Multi-model in one conversation.** Chat with ChatGPT, switch to Claude, then Gemini—same thread. Whatever they write, you can edit. No copy-paste between tabs.
2. **It never forgets.** Everything you talk about gets documented. Context lives in your project—no new-chat amnesia.
3. **Ask mode.** Turn it on and the AI can't touch anything—just feedback, like a regular chat. No accidental edits.
4. **Cursor under the hood.** It's a technical tool; we stripped most of that from sight. But it's all still there. Plugins and extensions can expand capabilities for non-technical users without ever needing another product.
5. **You never lose anything.** Every keystroke saved, versioned, backed up to GitHub. No more "Notion ate my draft" or "Google Docs sync failed."
6. **Your files are always yours.** Plain Markdown in a folder. No lock-in. Open them anywhere, anytime. If Zendoc disappeared tomorrow, your words are still there.
7. **One place for everything.** Notes, drafts, blog posts—all in one workspace. No juggling Notion + Docs + Word. The AI sees the whole project.
8. **The AI sees your project.** Not a generic chat. It knows your files, your structure, your history. That's why it doesn't forget what you're working on.
9. **Completely Free.** It was built because it needs to exist.
10. **Collaborate with AI. **On your machine, not the cloud.

## Hooks (options to test)

- **Your AI forgets. This one doesn't.** — Direct, contrasts with ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude.
- **Where your AI actually remembers.** — Softer, benefit-led.
- **Ever asked an AI something and it had no idea what you were talking about?** — Question hook, relatable.
- **One conversation. Every model. Your project.** — Feature-led, for people who juggle models.
- **Your words. Never lost. Never forgotten.** — Poetic, alliterative.
